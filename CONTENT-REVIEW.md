# Content review and launch checklist

## Design decisions
This version uses an English default entry, a dedicated Georgian page for each English page, a black/ivory/red editorial aesthetic, and a music-first homepage. The images are remote publisher photographs and music-video thumbnails, not generated photographs. Rights and exact crops must be confirmed with the artist. The duet without a verified image uses a typographic placeholder. The biography is an editorial draft rather than an approved artist statement. A simple static HTML/CSS/JS architecture was chosen to minimize hosting and maintenance needs.
