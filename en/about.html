The artist

More than a voice.

Over two decades of music, stage and creative exploration.

GOGA MESKHI / გოგა მესხი

Singer. Actor. Performer.

Georgian vocalist and actor bringing new life to familiar songs while developing his own repertoire. His journey spans vocal ensembles, television, orchestral stages and contemporary studio recordings.

Discover the music ↗

From theatre to music

Raised in a musical family, Goga initially studied dentistry before switching to theatre. He trained as an actor and later made music his career. In 2003 he joined the vocal group Vivo, which performed live and released an album.

Source ↗

Television and the stage

Star Academy introduced him to a broad television audience. In 2013 he joined Maia Darsmelidze’s team on The Voice of Georgia; later he appeared in GDS’s One to One.

Source ↗

Orchestral collaborations

His work with Vakhtang Kakhidze includes performances alongside the Tbilisi Symphony Orchestra. In August 2019 he was part of the Black Sea Arena concert celebrating Jansugh and Vakhtang Kakhidze’s music, with ensemble Rustavi and fellow Georgian vocalists.

Source ↗

A contemporary repertoire

From the 2011 video Nu Damemdurebi to recent recordings, Goga builds a repertoire of original songs and reinterpretations. His collaboration with Keti Chanturidze produced Davbrundebi in 2021; in 2023 he launched another duet project with Nanuka Karukhnishvili.

Source ↗

Music and cinema

His recent visual projects revisit Georgian classics while bringing together music, poetry and film. In a February 2026 radio interview, he also described his ambition to make a film.
