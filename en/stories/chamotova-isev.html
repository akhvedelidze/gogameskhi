A familiar song, a new vision

A new interpretation of Chamotova Isev.

A reinterpretation of a Georgian composition by Mamuka Charkviani, with poetry by Jansugh Charkviani and a new visual treatment. The official video was published in January 2025.

In an interview, Goga recalled hearing Mamuka Charkviani’s version and later asking permission to record his own interpretation. His video continues that creative conversation.
