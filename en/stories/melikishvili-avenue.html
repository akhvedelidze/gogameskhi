A song for Tbilisi

The story of Melikishvili Avenue.

A tribute to Goga’s friend, Georgian singer Zura Doijashvili. The recording connects a familiar Tbilisi street with the memories and music of the city.

In an interview with Radio Fortuna, Goga described the track as a special part of his repertoire because of its dedication to Doijashvili. The official video, presented as part of Georgian Stories, brings the listener into the cultural landscape of Tbilisi.
